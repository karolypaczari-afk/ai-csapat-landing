Frequently asked questions

Is this for me?

How do I know which specialist to call?

You don't – and you don't need to. You describe the job to the team lead, in your own words: “I want to advertise my coffee shop.” It puts the team together for you: it picks the specialists the job needs and the account connections that go with them, puts them in the order that works best, and sees the work through – where a job takes more than one pass, it runs the same specialist again. Your only job is to say what you want to achieve.

I am not an AI person. Will this work for me?

Yes. There is no coding and no prompt engineering: you talk to the specialists in chat, in your own words, the way you would brief a colleague. They ask you for what they need to know – your job is to answer, not to phrase a command. Installation comes with an illustrated walkthrough, and if you get stuck, write to us and we will go through it with you.

I already work with an agency. Does this still make sense?

Yes, and not instead of them. Start with the audit: the team goes through your ad accounts, your tracking and your emails, and shows you what made money and what did not. That does not make your agency worse – it makes you a better client, because you know exactly what to ask for. What you keep in-house and what you outsource stays your call.

Does it only advise, or does it do the work?

That depends on the package. Planner researches, audits, plans and writes the copy; execution stays with you. Autopilot can also use supported connectors in accounts you choose. Changes start paused or in draft, are read back after execution and go live only under the approval setting you select.

What do you need?

Do I need a Claude account? Is a free one enough?

One AI account is enough, and you can start on a free plan. Claude free: you can upload our specialists (Settings → Capabilities → turn on “Code execution and file creation”, then Customize → Skills), and it also allows one custom connector – which is exactly how many we need. So a free account carries the whole team and the connection. Why you might still pay: Claude Pro gives you more allowance, not more capability, while Cowork and Claude Code require a paid Claude plan – neither is needed to get started.

Does it work with both Claude and ChatGPT?

Yes – the specialists work with both Claude and ChatGPT. We built them on Claude because that is where the specialist experience is best, and where a free plan already carries both the upload and the single custom connector we need. You are not locked in: ChatGPT (and Codex) can convert our specialists to work there too, on their own. If you get stuck, write to us at info@genmarketer.hu and we will help you through it.

How do I install it? How long does it take?

About ten minutes, by clicking. Every specialist comes with two kinds of guide: a simple upload for Claude, Cowork or ChatGPT, and a command for Claude Code – both with an illustrated walkthrough. If you get stuck at any point, write to us and we will go through it with you; if it is easier that way, we set it up together on a call.

Do I really use it in my language?

Yes. Each specialist runs inside your own AI (Claude or ChatGPT), so it reads your brief and replies in whichever language you write in. The specialists, the written guide and the examples are all in English.

What does Claude cost on top of this?

Nothing is compulsory. A free Claude account is enough to start and to produce your first pieces of work. For heavier workloads Claude Pro (around 20 dollars a month) is more comfortable – it gives you more allowance, not more capability – and Cowork and Claude Code require a paid plan. Whatever you pay for your AI account goes to Anthropic or OpenAI directly; it is not part of any GENmarketer plan.

Do I need my own website or ad account?

Not to use the team – research, plans, copy and creative only need your own AI account. The Autopilot connectors do need them: that is where it works, inside your own Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4 or MailerLite account. If one of them does not exist yet, we can help you set it up – and until then that specialist hands you a plan instead, while the rest of the team carries on.

Will I run out of my Claude allowance?

An ad copy set, a landing page plan or a competitor table fits inside the free allowance, because the team lead only starts the specialists a task actually needs rather than the whole team. On heavy days Claude Pro or Claude Code is more comfortable, but you do not need either to start. On what it costs: a subscription is not billed per token – see the pricing section for the detail.

Do I need a paid MailerLite plan?

Not necessarily. The email specialist writes and assembles your sequences on the free plan too. It will not talk around it.

How does it work?

What exactly does the team lead do?

It is the conductor, and it does four things. It learns your business once – about five questions, and from then on every specialist reads that, so you never have to explain what you do again. It assembles the team for the job: it picks the relevant specialists and the account connections they need, from the live list rather than from memory – and it tells you if a connector is not connected yet. It puts them in order: for a bigger goal you get a sequence rather than a single answer – customer profile, ad plan, ad copy, creative, landing page plan, measurement – and it says who goes first and who builds on that. Finally, it sees the work through: where a job takes more than one pass it runs the same specialist again, and hands the result to the next one. You never need to know a single specialist's name.

Do they always work from the latest knowledge?

Yes. For every task the team lead pulls that specialist's current material live, rather than working from its own memory. So when we improve a specialist – a new platform rule, a new way of measuring, a better method – it is there for you straight away, with nothing to reinstall. That is the point of the subscription.

How long until I have my first finished piece of work?

Installation usually takes about ten minutes, although the exact time depends on your platform and account. You can start your first brief immediately afterwards; completion time depends on the task, the source material and the review it needs.

How do I know that what it tells me is true?

That is a hard rule for us: every specialist has to say when it does not have the data, instead of inventing it – and to name its own limits. Where numbers matter – ad performance, keywords, list data – it works from the real data in your own account, not from memory. And Autopilot reads back every change it makes.

How is this different from Meta's and Google's built-in AI?

They can help with tasks inside their own platforms, but they work differently. GENmarketer is designed to coordinate research, strategy, copy, creative direction and supported account work across a wider workflow, using the business context you provide. Connector actions are read back so you can verify the resulting state.

How is this more than a prompt collection?

The difference is maintenance. Behind the specialists sits collected expert knowledge, wired-up account connectors and continuous market tracking that we keep current – and the ad platforms change every month. A prompt collection stops where it was written; this team moves on with you. Build it yourself and you are not paying for prompts, you are paying with your own time.

I already have a reporting tool. How is this different?

A reporting tool reads: it lays out the numbers and leaves the conclusion to you. The team reads the same numbers, tells you what they mean and what to do about it – and with Autopilot it makes the fix in the same conversation. Our reporting ends where most tools would have you start working.

What happens to my accounts?

What exactly does Autopilot do in my accounts?

It can audit supported Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4, Search Console and MailerLite accounts, prepare campaigns or email sequences and implement supported changes. New campaign assets start paused or in draft unless your current connector approval explicitly allows publication. After every write it reads the result back so you can verify what changed.

Can I manage several ad accounts? What if I log in as a manager on someone else’s account?

One licence is one login – effectively one e-mail address, one person. But that one person can manage any number of ad accounts. When you connect, you authorise your own Meta and Google login, and whatever that login can reach, the team can work on: your own accounts, and also the client accounts you reach through a Business portfolio (Meta) or a manager account (Google Ads MCC). If you look after several companies’ accounts, that does not need extra licences – it only needs your login to see them. While you work you always name the account in question, and the team works account by account: two clients’ data never mixes, and approval stays a separate question per account. You need a second licence when a colleague wants their own login – account connections are tied to a person. Pick one to five seats next to the packages.

Who decides on spending?

You do. Your daily and campaign-level spending limits stay where they are today – in your own ad account – and the team works inside them. There is no wallet on our side that it could reach. You also choose how it works: for each connector you can approve one exact execution, or grant approval-free operation until you revoke it, and you can revoke connector access at any time. New ad assets start paused by default.

Who connects my accounts?

You grant the access yourself, but you are not left alone with it. It takes about two minutes per connector, by clicking, on the provider's own sign-in page (Meta, Google, MailerLite) – so you never hand us a password. If you get stuck on any of them, tell us and we will do it together: we walk you through it, or we set it up with you on a call.

What happens to my data?

The specialists run inside your own AI account – your briefs and your copy are created there and stay there. For account connections you grant the access yourself, per connector, and you can revoke it at any time; the access only covers the account you picked. Details are in our Privacy Notice.
