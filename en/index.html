Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Claude account?

Yes. The specialists come in Claude skill format. Claude's free tier is enough for many tasks; for heavier use, Claude Pro or Claude Code is recommended. Cowork and the Claude Code CLI are both supported.

Does it work with both Claude and ChatGPT?

Yes – the specialists work with both Claude and ChatGPT. We built them on Claude because that's where the specialist experience is best, but you're not locked in: ChatGPT (and Codex) can convert our specialists to work there too, on their own. If you get stuck, the Consultation package includes hands-on help.

What if I can't install it?

Every lesson includes two kinds of guide: a simple upload to Cowork, and a command for Claude Code. If you get stuck, there's an illustrated walkthrough. And in the Consultation package we set it up together with you.

Do I really use it in my language?

Yes. Each specialist works inside your own AI (Claude or ChatGPT), which understands and replies in your language. The training material, the examples and the guides are all there too, tuned to your market.

What does Claude cost on top of the course?

Claude's free tier gives you a limited number of messages – enough for many tasks. For more, Claude Pro (about 7 800 Ft / month) or Claude Code is recommended. You pay this directly to Anthropic; it's not part of the course price.

Is there customer support?

Yes. If you get stuck with anything – installation, usage, billing – you can write to us and we'll help. And in the Training + Consultation package we sit down with you in person to get started.

How long does the team keep updating?

What you buy stays yours for good. We make a concrete update promise for at least six months from your purchase: new specialists keep arriving, and existing ones keep improving – largely based on buyers' feedback.

What's the difference between the two packages?

Both include the full team. The Consultation package adds 2 hours of personal consultation with Tibor and his team: together we set up the specialists, and answer all your questions live.

Why an introductory price?

Because the team keeps growing. As new specialists arrive, the price goes up. Whoever joins now locks in the full team at today's price, and gets continuous updates for at least 6 months.
