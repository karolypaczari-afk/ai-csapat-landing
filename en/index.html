Frequently asked questions

Is this for me?

I am not an AI person. Will this work for me?

Yes. There is no coding and no prompt engineering: you talk to the specialists in chat, in your own words, the way you would brief a colleague. They ask you for what they need to know – your job is to answer, not to phrase a command. Installation comes with an illustrated walkthrough, and if you get stuck, write to us.

I already work with an agency. Does this still make sense?

Yes, and not instead of them. Start with the audit: the team goes through your ad accounts, your tracking and your emails, and shows you what made money and what did not. That does not make your agency worse – it makes you a better client, because you know exactly what to ask for. What you keep in-house and what you outsource stays your call.

Does it only advise, or does it do the work?

That depends on the package, and it is the main difference. Planner researches, audits, plans and writes the copy – execution stays with you. Autopilot also does the work inside your own Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4 and MailerLite accounts: it builds the campaign, adjusts it as it goes, and takes it live.

What do you need?

Do I need a Claude account?

Yes. The specialists come in Claude skill format. Claude's free tier is enough for many tasks; for heavier use, Claude Pro or Claude Code is recommended. Cowork and the Claude Code CLI are both supported.

Does it work with both Claude and ChatGPT?

Yes – the specialists work with both Claude and ChatGPT. We built them on Claude because that's where the specialist experience is best, but you're not locked in: ChatGPT (and Codex) can convert our specialists to work there too, on their own. If you get stuck, write to us and we'll help you through it.

What if I can't install it?

Every specialist comes with two kinds of guide: a simple upload for Claude or ChatGPT, and a command for Claude Code. If you get stuck, there's an illustrated walkthrough – and you can always write to us.

Do I really use it in my language?

Yes. Each specialist runs inside your own AI (Claude or ChatGPT), so it reads your brief and replies in whichever language you write in. The specialists, the written guide and the examples are all in English.

What does Claude cost on top of this?

Claude's free tier gives you a limited number of messages – enough for many tasks. For more, Claude Pro (about €20 / month) or Claude Code is recommended. You pay this directly to Anthropic; it's not part of what you pay us.

Do I need my own website or ad account?

Not to use the team – research, plans, copy and creative only need your own AI account. The Autopilot connectors do need them: that is where it works, inside your own Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4 or MailerLite account. If one of them does not exist yet, that specialist hands you a plan instead, and the rest of the team carries on.

Will I run out of my Claude allowance?

On heavy days the free tier will not be enough – that is why we recommend Claude Pro or Claude Code. In practice the thing that helps most is running one specialist per task rather than the whole team: they hand work to each other, they do not all work on it in parallel.

Do I need a paid MailerLite plan?

Not necessarily. The email specialist writes and assembles your sequences on the free plan too. A few of MailerLite’s more advanced automations live on their paid tiers – when that happens, the specialist either suggests a way around it or tells you plainly that this one needs a plan upgrade. It will not talk around it.

How does it work?

How long until I have my first finished piece of work?

Installation takes about ten minutes. Your first finished output – a set of ad copy, a landing page plan, a competitor table – is done the same evening. You are not buying a course you have to watch first: you hand over the first task in the first conversation.

What if the AI makes something up?

That is a hard rule for us: every specialist has to say when it does not have the data, instead of inventing it – and to name its own limits. Where numbers matter – ad performance, keywords, list data – it works from the real data in your own account, not from memory. And Autopilot reads back every change it makes.

Don't Meta and Google's own AI do this for free?

Partly, but differently. Google’s built-in AI lists what could be improved; Meta’s walks you through a wizard step by step. Neither one knows your business, and neither takes a campaign all the way from research through copy and creative to the actual setup. The team does that in one pass, and reads back every change it makes.

Why not just assemble this myself from free prompts?

You can – plenty of people try. The difference is maintenance: behind the specialists sits collected expert knowledge, wired-up account connectors and continuous market tracking that we keep current. Build it yourself and you are not paying for prompts, you are paying with your own time – and the ad platforms change every month.

I already have a reporting tool. How is this different?

A reporting tool reads: it lays out the numbers and leaves the conclusion to you. The team reads the same numbers, tells you what they mean and what to do about it – and with Autopilot it makes the fix in the same conversation. Our reporting ends where most tools would have you start working.

What happens to my accounts?

What exactly does Autopilot do in my accounts?

It carries the work through rather than commenting on it. It audits your Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4 and MailerLite accounts, reports on them, builds the campaign or the email sequence, adjusts it as it goes, and takes it live. After every write it reads the result back, so you are not trusting that it "probably went through" – you see what it turned into.

Can it spend my money? Can I stop it?

By default the Autopilot connectors run in approval-free mode: they do not stop at every single step to ask – without that, no campaign could actually be carried through. If you would rather be asked, one sentence in chat is enough: "ask me for approval on the Meta connector" – from then on it stops before every go-live. This is set per connector, at any time, in both directions. Your spending limits stay where you set them, in your own ad account: the team works inside them.

What happens to my data?

The specialists run inside your own AI account – your briefs and your copy are created there and stay there. For account connections you grant the access yourself, per connector, and you can revoke it at any time; the access only covers the account you picked. Details are in our Privacy Notice.
