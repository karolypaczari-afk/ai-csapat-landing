Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Claude account?

Yes. The specialists come in Claude skill format. Claude's free tier is enough for many tasks; for heavier use, Claude Pro or Claude Code is recommended. Cowork and the Claude Code CLI are both supported.

Does it work with both Claude and ChatGPT?

Yes – the specialists work with both Claude and ChatGPT. We built them on Claude because that's where the specialist experience is best, but you're not locked in: ChatGPT (and Codex) can convert our specialists to work there too, on their own. If you get stuck, write to us and we'll help you through it.

What if I can't install it?

Every specialist comes with two kinds of guide: a simple upload for Claude or ChatGPT, and a command for Claude Code. If you get stuck, there's an illustrated walkthrough – and you can always write to us.

Do I really use it in my language?

Yes. Each specialist runs inside your own AI (Claude or ChatGPT), so it reads your brief and replies in whichever language you write in. The specialists, the written guide and the examples are all in English.

What does Claude cost on top of this?

Claude's free tier gives you a limited number of messages – enough for many tasks. For more, Claude Pro (about €20 / month) or Claude Code is recommended. You pay this directly to Anthropic; it's not part of what you pay us.

Is there customer support?

Yes. If you get stuck with anything – installation, usage, billing – you can write to us and we'll help, in English.

How long does the team keep updating?

On a subscription, for as long as you subscribe: new specialists appear automatically and the existing ones keep improving – largely based on subscribers' feedback. On the one-off Training package we make a concrete update promise for at least six months from your purchase, and what you bought stays yours for good.

What's the difference between the packages?

All three include the full 28-strong team. Planner (€29/month) researches, audits your accounts, plans the campaign and writes the copy – execution stays with you. Autopilot (€59/month) also connects to your own Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4, Search Console and MailerLite accounts, builds the campaign and, with your approval, takes it live. Training (€189 once) is the same team bought outright: no monthly fee and no connectors, with at least six months of updates.

Will my price go up?

Not while you stay subscribed. The team keeps growing, and the list price rises with it – but the rate you joined at is the rate you keep. It only changes if you cancel and come back later. The one-off Training package has no recurring price at all – you pay once, at the price shown on the day you buy.

What if it's not for me after all?

On a subscription: cancel and you stop paying – there is no minimum term and no notice period. You keep access to the end of the month you have already paid for, and cancelling takes one click from the link in your welcome e-mail. On the one-off Training package you have a 30-day money-back guarantee – one e-mail is enough.

Can I switch between Planner and Autopilot?

Yes, in either direction, from your subscription page. The change applies from your next monthly payment – nothing is charged twice. If you bought the one-off Training package, you can subscribe later at any time to keep the team growing with you.
